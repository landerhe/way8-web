Welcome to the Way8 Support Center. Way8 is a mindful pause utility designed to help you interrupt habitual app-opening and reclaim intentional control over your screen time. Below are answers to common questions and setup assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I configure Way8 with iOS Shortcuts?
Setting up Way8 takes less than two minutes:
1. Open Way8 and choose the apps you would like to be mindful of under "Choose apps".
2. Tap "Shortcuts connection" and import the official "Way8 Helper Shortcut" template from iCloud.
3. In the iOS Shortcuts app, verify that the automation trigger is set to Run Immediately.
4. When you open any of your monitored apps, Way8 presents a mindful pause before you proceed.
2. Why didn't an intervention trigger when I opened an app?
Please check the following:
• Master Toggle: Ensure the main intervention switch is turned ON in Way8.
• Individual App Toggle: Ensure the specific target app is toggled on in "My Apps".
• Grace Period: If you recently tapped "Continue", the app grants a temporary grace period (default 60s) so your normal usage isn't repeatedly interrupted.
• Automation Status: Open the iOS Shortcuts app and verify that the automation is active and enabled.
3. How does Paced Breathing work?
Way8 features a scientifically proven 4-second inhale and 6-second exhale cycle (6 breaths per minute) to help calm physiological arousal and break impulsive loops. You can adjust the breathing pace, configure extra rounds for repeated opens, or skip breathing at any moment.
4. How do Alternative Destinations work?
In Way8 Settings, you can configure up to two healthy alternative apps (such as Books, Podcasts, or a habit tracker). During a pause, you can tap directly to jump to one of these apps instead of continuing into doomscrolling.
5. Is my personal usage tracked or sent to the cloud?
No. Way8 is 100% on-device. There are no user accounts, no analytics tracking frameworks, and no external servers receiving your app usage logs.
