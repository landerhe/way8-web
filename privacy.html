Privacy Policy

Way8 • Operated by Shangrao Tongtu Technology Co., Ltd.
Core Commitment: Way8 is built from the ground up as a 100% on-device mindfulness and mindful pause utility. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or sell any of your personal data or application activity.

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy applies to the Way8 mobile application ("the App"), provided and operated by Shangrao Tongtu Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy outlines our strict commitment to on-device privacy and explains how data is handled when you use the App.

2. On-Device Data Storage

All data generated by or configured within Way8 is stored exclusively inside your iOS device’s local, secure application sandbox. This includes:

  • Monitored Applications: The local identifiers and names of apps you choose to configure for mindful pauses.
  • Custom Rules: Your customized intervention settings (such as repeat-opening window duration, breathing rhythm speed, and grace periods).
  • Conscious Choice Records: Timestamps of locally observed app open requests, whether you tapped "Continue" or "Not now", and completed breathing sessions.
  • Alternative Destinations: Your selected productive redirect apps (e.g. Books, Podcasts, or Safari).

This data is strictly utilized on-device to render your personal practice trends and execute your chosen pause rules. We operate no cloud databases, telemetric backends, or data collection infrastructure.

3. Zero Third-Party Trackers & No Account Required

Way8 is designed to respect your digital sovereignty:

  • No Account: You are never asked to register, log in, or provide any email address or telephone number to use the App.
  • No Advertising: There are no ad networks, tracking pixels, or monetization SDKs integrated into the App.
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate Google Firebase, Meta SDK, ByteDance SDK, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, or any other telemetry libraries.
  • No Inspection of Target Content: Way8 operates via system-level triggers and cannot inspect, read, or intercept private messages, photos, or browsing content inside your other apps.

4. Apple System Services & Shortcuts

Way8 integrates with native Apple iOS Shortcuts and App Intents:

  • Shortcuts templates are hosted securely on Apple’s official iCloud infrastructure. Downloading templates connects directly to Apple services under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • If you enable iOS iCloud Backup on your device, local app state may be included in your encrypted device backup managed by Apple.

5. Data Retention & User Control

You retain full authority over your data at all times:

  • Instant Clear: You can purge all historical statistics and choice counts at any time in Settings > Data Management.
  • Export Records: You can export your configuration and event records to a standard JSON file via the iOS Files app.
  • Complete Removal: Deleting the Way8 app from your device immediately erases all sandbox data stored by the App.
